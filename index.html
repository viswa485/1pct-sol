FOUR HUMAN PATCH NOTES

  • Map your tasks. Notice repetitive work, research, drafting and admin that tools might help with.
  • Try one workflow. Use AI to assist a real task instead of collecting another dozen subscriptions.
  • Check the output. Verify facts, sources, calculations and anything important before relying on it.
  • Keep your human edge. Build judgement, communication, context, creativity and accountability.
